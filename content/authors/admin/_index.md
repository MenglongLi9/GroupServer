Prof. Jianghua Shen's research interests include: (1) Evaluation of impact resistance, fatigue resistance, and creep resistance mechanical properties of advanced metal materials, mechanical performance evaluation and service life evaluation of structures, mechanical performance testing technology, etc.; (2) Constitutive relations of aerospace structural materials, structural finite element simulation and structural strength design, data-driven structural mechanical performance evaluation, etc.; (3) Analysis of the micromechanical mechanism of fracture and fatigue failure of metal materials or structures, research on material structure characterization and damage evolution rules, including failure of materials or structures under extreme service conditions (high/low temperature, high strain rate, corrosion, etc.) analyze; (4) Research on advanced manufacturing process technologies such as laser surface treatment and plastic processing, as well as research on powder metallurgy preparation processes such as additive manufacturing.
